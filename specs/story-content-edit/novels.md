## Novels

### Outline: 
A novel has a basic structure of Books and Chapters
The user should be able to manage the current story's books and book's chapters introducing text to organize their story

### Stucture
Story -> Books -> Chapters

### UX
- Allow navigation and management to individual books or chapters from the content page
- From the Book page the user can change its name and access its chapters or read them.
- In the Content and Book pages display the books and chapters with an accordiaon-like mechanism to not overwhel the user
- Allow the user to re-sort Books to enable prequels writen after a book is already created
- In the content page allow a search by Book title to access content easily
